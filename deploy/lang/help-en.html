1. What this is and the two interfaces

An electronic blood pressure diary: measurements, a symptom diary, medications and reminders. A personal neural network learns from your data and explains the blood pressure forecast (reaction to weather, medications, sleep deprivation).

💻 App🌐 Web bp.xor.world

Datastored in a folder next to the programstored on the server, one account per person
Reminder soundWindows system sound, always workssound in the tab + browser push notifications
Special featuresport configuration, sync with the webdevices and tokens, quick card "/q", install on your phone

You can keep your data in both places at once and synchronize them (section 12).

2. Getting started

💻 App

  1. Download the installer BpMonitorSetup.exe (on the site — the "⬇ Desktop version" button in the "Devices" section, or "⬇ Desktop version" on bp.xor.world) and run it — reinstalling does not touch your data.
  2. After launch, a ❤ icon appears in the system tray and the interface opens in your browser. If the browser did not open — double-click the tray icon.
  3. Go to "Settings → User" and enter your date of birth and gender — the model uses them as features.
  4. In "Settings → General", check your city coordinates (needed for the weather).

🌐 Web

  1. Open https://bp.xor.world and sign in with your email (an account is created automatically on first login; the password is your email password).
  2. Then — the same "Settings → User" and city coordinates.

3. Measuring blood pressure

  1. Sit with your arm on the table and rest for 5 minutes. Take a measurement with your tonometer.
  2. Click ⚡ measure (top right). The current time is filled in automatically — you can adjust it.
  3. Enter the systolic/diastolic pressure and pulse. Check off the medications you took (if any) and add a note.
  4. The weather, geomagnetic conditions and diary entries for that time are picked up automatically.

🌐 Web There is a quick card /q (the "⚡ quick" button) — three fields and nothing else, convenient from a phone.

Any measurement can be edited: open it from the "History" tab — editing keeps a change history.

4. History and charts

  • The History tab — a table of measurements (newest on top) and a log. The "Show more" button loads older entries.
  • Charts: pressure/pulse over time, dot coloring by emotion. The date filter is above the chart.
  • "Doctor's report" — see section 13.

5. Diary: emotions, noise, sleep

Three short entries help the model understand why your pressure changed:

  • Emotion — from −10 (terrible) to +10 (excellent);
  • Noise/events — what interfered (coffee, stress, physical exertion…);
  • Sleep — hours and quality (1–10).

An entry made within ~12 hours before a measurement (or within 10 minutes after it) is linked to it automatically. Fill them in at least on days when you feel unwell — the model will appreciate it.

6. Medications

  • List — the medications you take. They appear as checkboxes in a measurement and are used in the model's forecast (time of intake and half-life are taken into account).
  • Reminders — intake times for each medication; a reminder arrives in the tray (💻) or as a push (🌐).
  • Intake log — the "✓ Take" button records the fact of intake (more accurate for the model than the checkbox next to a measurement).
  • PK reference — for each medication you can set the half-life/duration of action (by default — taken from a medical reference).

7. Reminders

The Reminders tab — a list with quick inline editing. Modes:

  • every day — at the specified time;
  • on weekdays — check boxes Mon–Sun;
  • every N hours — e.g. "every 8 hours" from the moment it was enabled.

"Remind N min ahead" — an early warning. When it goes off, the melody alarm.mp3 plays; to stop it — the "⏹ sound" button in the header or a click on the tray icon.

🌐 Web If a tab is open — it will ring (once, without duplicating across other tabs). If there are no open tabs — a push notification arrives (section 8). If the browser blocks sound until a click — just click anywhere on the page once.

8. Phone: web app and notifications 🌐

Install as an app

  1. Open bp.xor.world in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iOS) and sign in.
  2. "Settings → Devices → 📲 Install on phone" (or the browser menu "Install app" / "Add to Home Screen").
  3. After installation the site opens as a regular app, without an address bar.

Enable push notifications

  1. In "Settings", in the "Notifications" block, click Enable and allow the browser's prompt.
  2. The button shows the status of this particular browser; "📡 Delivery test" sends a test to all subscribed devices.
  3. On iPhone, push works only from the Safari PWA installed on the device.

Notifications arrive for: reminders (when no tabs are open), medication reminders, and model warnings.

9. Neural network and forecast

The Model tab. The neural network learns to predict your blood pressure from: time of day, weather (pressure, temperature, humidity, geomagnetic disturbances), emotions/noise/sleep, medications (taking intake time into account), as well as gender and age at the time of measurement.

  • Train — regular training; Genetics — more thorough (background task, ~half a minute); Tune — automatic model-size selection; Validate — an honest evaluation on the held-out most recent measurements (MAE in mmHg) and the "contribution" of features.
  • After any significant data change (new measurement, medication, weather) the model retrains on its own.
  • Forecast — the model is run over the hourly weather forecast and shows when a departure from your normal range is expected; at that point a "in ~N hours" warning also arrives.
  • The Kp badge — the forecast of geomagnetic disturbances (≥5 — magnetic storms; sensitive people react with blood pressure changes).
The model is an assistant, not a diagnosis. It explains patterns in your data but does not replace a doctor.

10. Weather and geomagnetic conditions

Below the header the current weather and Kp are shown — they are filled into every measurement automatically (source — the Norwegian meteorological service for the app and the site, GFZ/NOAA — for Kp). Missing weather for past days can be backfilled: the "Model" tab → "Backfill weather" (💻) — the archive is pulled in by measurement dates.

11. Settings

The Settings tab — cards; "Edit" opens an editing window, everything is saved immediately.

  • Normal range — your systolic/diastolic thresholds (for highlighting, forecasts and the report).
  • User — name, date of birth, gender (important for the model!).
  • General — city coordinates, weather polling interval; 💻 web interface port (changing it requires a restart).
  • 🌐 Time zone — for reminders and charts.
  • 🌐 Devices — sync tokens for the app/scripts, the install-on-phone button, "⬇ Desktop version".
  • 💻 Synchronization — section 12.
  • Model — fine-tuning parameters of the neural network (usually no need to change).

The dark theme is enabled automatically based on the system setting.

12. Syncing the app with the site 💻↔🌐

To keep the diary on both your computer and your phone without manual transfers:

  1. 🌐 On the site: "Settings → Devices → Create token". Copy the token (it is shown only once).
  2. 💻 In the app: "Settings → Synchronization": enable it, paste the token, save. After that, once a minute (or immediately after a new measurement) changes go to the server and come back.
  3. Which side is "master" in settings (who dictates the configuration to whom) is also chosen in this block: "desktop" / "server" / "do not synchronize".

The status is visible in the same block ("push +N / pull +N"). The "invalid token" error — create a new token.

13. Export, doctor's report, backups

  • Doctor's report ("History" → "Doctor's report", CSV or xlsx) — a per-day summary: measurements, medication intake, Kp, emotions, noise, sleep. The file opens in Excel.
  • Export all data — a single JSON file with the entire diary (for moving to another computer: use "Import" at the new location). Before importing, an automatic backup snapshot is taken.
  • 💻 The app itself keeps daily backups in the backups\ folder next to the program (the last 30 days).
  • 🌐 Server data is backed up by the site administrator.

14. Frequently asked questions

The reminder rang twice / did not ring
Twice — check whether several tabs and the app are open at the same time (tabs coordinate among themselves, the app rings on its own). Did not ring on the phone — the browser blocked sound before the first click: open the tab and click; a push arrives only if the tabs are closed and the subscription is enabled ("📡 Delivery test" in settings).
Push does not arrive
"Settings → Notifications" → "Enable" → allow the prompt. Seeing an "Disable" button instead of "Enable" on an empty phone? Reload the page — the button now applies to the current browser. On iOS — Safari only + install to the "Home" screen.
Weather was not filled into a measurement
Check the coordinates in settings and internet access. For old measurements — "Backfill weather".
Synchronization reports "Task canceled"
No connection to the server (or a proxy/VPN is interfering). Check whether bp.xor.world opens in the browser on this computer, then wait for the next cycle — the status will update.
Forgot my password (web)
The password is your email password (the mail server itself is the verification). Reset the password using your email provider's tools.
Where are my data?
💻 in the folder next to BpMonitor.exe (data\), never sent anywhere except the synchronization you yourself enabled. 🌐 on the bp.xor.world server, accessible only under your account.
